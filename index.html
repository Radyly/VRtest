Custom anime-inspired 3D world based on your campus video. Location: Welcome Hall.

Customize the world
Desktop: click scene and use WASD + mouse to walk, 1–5 to teleport. Quest 3: select Enter VR, left stick moves, right stick snap-turns, A/B changes zones. Point your controller at glowing teleport pads and press trigger. VR browser requires HTTPS.
Prototype scope: Procedural 3D rooms and exhibits; simplified movement boundaries. No multiplayer or detailed robot interaction yet.
